Add `warp.optim.linear.KrylovSolve`, a prepared zero-start solve of one system for several right-hand sides with `cg()`, `cr()`, `bicgstab()` or `gmres()`, selected by `warp.optim.linear.KrylovMethod`. It keeps per-RHS and cumulative diagnostics on device, supports CUDA graph replay, mixed working precision for sparse matrices, and `warp.optim.linear.ResidualMode` to choose recomputed or recurrence residuals. `warp.optim.linear.MultiRHSOperator` repeats one operator over independent right-hand sides.
