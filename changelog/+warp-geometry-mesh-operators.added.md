Added differentiable simplicial mesh operators to ``warp.geometry`` for triangle and tetrahedral meshes: ``laplacian`` (cotangent and uniform, with ``row_compress`` and ``triplets`` backends), ``massmatrix`` (barycentric and mixed-Voronoi lumping), ``harmonic`` (``k``-harmonic and biharmonic solves following ``igl::harmonic``), ``dec_operators`` (the DEC exterior derivative ``d0`` and diagonal Hodge star ``star1`` factoring the Laplacian), ``vertex_adjacency_matrix``, and ``MetricHarmonicSolver`` (a matrix-free harmonic solve over the edge metric with an adjoint gradient). A ``geometry.example_bounded_biharmonic_weights`` example computes Wang & Solomon-style quasi-harmonic skinning weights from these.
