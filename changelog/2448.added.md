Add `warp.optim.eigen` with reusable LOBPCG and symmetric Krylov–Schur solvers for real float64 standard and SPD generalized partial eigenproblems on CPU and CUDA, prepared CUDA graph composition, device-resident accuracy diagnostics, and caller-supplied iterative inverse/preconditioning actions. CUDA LOBPCG uses coalesced sparse products for wide vector-valued FEM blocks.
