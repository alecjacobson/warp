Add surface sampling on triangle meshes to `warp.geometry`. `uniformly_sample()` and
`UniformSampler` draw points spread evenly by area, and the `draw_sample()` device function
draws them inside your own kernels. `poisson_disk_sample()` and `PoissonDiskSampler` draw
points no closer than a given radius, measured in 3D or approximately along the surface with
`geodesic=True`, and `pair_correlation()` measures how the samples are spaced. Both samplers
have an `update()` method for meshes whose vertices move, which can be captured in a CUDA
graph.
