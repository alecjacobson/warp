Add surface sampling on triangle meshes to `warp.geometry`. `warp.geometry.uniformly_sample`
draws points spread evenly by area across a mesh, and `warp.geometry.UniformSampler` amortizes
the setup over many draws and accepts optional precomputed per-triangle areas (`face_areas`).
The device function `warp.geometry.draw_sample` samples a single point from within your own
kernels, returning a face index and barycentric coordinates like the `wp.mesh_query_*` builtins.

`warp.geometry.poisson_disk_sample` and `warp.geometry.PoissonDiskSampler` draw a blue-noise
point set in which no two samples are closer than a given radius, following Bowers et al.,
"Parallel Poisson Disk Sampling with Spectrum Analysis on Surfaces" (SIGGRAPH Asia 2010).
An optional `geodesic=True` flag measures the minimum distance along the surface using a
fast normal-based curvature correction, an approximation that is exact on a sphere, instead of
Euclidean distance. `warp.geometry.pair_correlation` measures the blue-noise spectrum of a
surface point set. See `warp/examples/geometry/`.

When the vertices of a mesh move but its triangles do not, `UniformSampler.update` and
`PoissonDiskSampler.update` rebuild the area distribution and the samples without host
synchronization or allocation, so they can be captured in a CUDA graph. Triangles are
selected from a double-precision area distribution with a 48-bit random number, which keeps
the selection probabilities accurate for meshes with tens of millions of triangles.
