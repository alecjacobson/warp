Add exact 3D point-cloud nearest-neighbor queries in kernels with `wp.point_query_nearest()`, `wp.point_query_knn()`, and `wp.point_query_next()`. Add `wp.geometry.PointIndex` and `wp.geometry.knn()` for reusable targets and independent fixed-size batches, differentiable distances, CUDA graph capture, and optional native cuBQL queries.
