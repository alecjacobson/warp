Add ``warp.geometry.swept_volume`` to extract the swept volume (motion envelope) of animated rigid meshes: a dense signed-distance field is stamped over the sampled poses and its zero isosurface is extracted with marching cubes. The companion ``warp.geometry.swept_volume_field`` returns the sampled field, and the ``warp.geometry.swept_volume_sdf`` device function is callable from user kernels. See ``warp/examples/geometry/example_swept_volume.py``.
