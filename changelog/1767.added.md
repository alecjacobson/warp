Add an initial `wp.indexed_sum` and `wp.summand` API for assembling the sparse Hessian of a summed local energy into
a `warp.sparse.BsrMatrix`. This first version covers a single `wp.array(dtype=wp.vec3)` variable with a hand-written
local Hessian over 1-, 2-, or 3-node stencils; see `design/sparse-hessians.md` and the
`optim/example_spring_hessian.py` example.
