Add `warp.geometry.oriented_bounding_box()` and `warp.geometry.oriented_bounding_boxes()` to fit approximate oriented
bounding boxes on the device, for one point set or for many packed back to back in a single batched call. The fit
minimizes volume, surface area, or mean width, with parameters to control the search, and supports CUDA graph capture.
Also add `warp.geometry.super_fibonacci()` for sampling rotations uniformly.
